# Validation metrics

## Experiment 1 — wedding spreadsheet competitor review mining

- **Status:** Desk-research sample completed; opportunity not validated.
- **Cost:** $0 planned; no spending authorized.
- **Sample:** 25 written reviews across seven direct wedding spreadsheet listings (most-recent publicly surfaced comments dated 2025–2026; not a random sample).
- **Record:** recurring user jobs, positive outcomes, usability friction, unresolved requests, and listing scope. Do not record reviewer identities.
- **Continue threshold:** At least five independent reviews indicate the same concrete unmet planning/payment-timing need, with no clear evidence the competitors already address it.
- **Pivot threshold:** A different unmet need appears repeatedly and has a plausible downloadable solution.
- **Stop threshold:** No consistent pain appears, or available free/paid tools already solve it adequately.
- **Actual results:** Most comments praise ease, usefulness, organization, and keeping planning information together. Sparse negative/mixed examples mention confusion, a missing checklist, and customization; the sample cannot show prevalence. Feature comparison found multiple products already advertising vendor payment dates, deposits/balances, or monthly cash-flow/shortfall views. The continuation threshold of five reviews identifying an underserved payment-timing problem was not met. Do not build or select this product on the current evidence.

No visits, conversions, purchases, revenue, refunds, support requests, or customer interviews have been recorded.
