# Project plan

## Status
The owner selected the Windows profile launcher as the next product experiment on 2026-10-20, while waiting for cat-content photos, and then authorized all five proposed features using sequential test-driven development. The Windows beta is implemented in [launcher/](launcher/README.md). Commercial demand is not validated.

## Current lead

Profile launcher for Windows users with separate work and personal Codex accounts. Implemented features cover guided profiles, live status/actions, project shortcuts, installation/diagnostics, and tray/hotkeys/single-instance behavior. See the [beta scope and validation plan](launcher/BETA_PLAN.md) and [test-first verification record](launcher/tests/TDD.md). No price, ads, payments, public publication or paid services have been approved.

## Previous research priorities (superseded by the launcher experiment)
1. Continue comparing opportunities against a specific, repeated unmet need and actual willingness-to-pay evidence.
2. Keep English as the default language for any next practical sample per the user's preference; test topic-level resonance separately, since market reach and purchasing-power evidence do not establish demand for a specific subject.
3. Find a narrow buyer job with repeated unmet-need evidence, then design a no-cost/manual value test before selecting a product format. Compare channels by current fees, payout eligibility, time-to-revenue barriers, recurring workload, and platform/rights constraints; the lore script is only a capability demonstration.
4. Only investigate a paid plan or paid video service after a chosen model has shown measurable value and the user chooses to proceed.

## Constraints
- The owner authorized work on the existing launcher as the next experiment; this supersedes the earlier research-only restriction for this scope. Avoid unrelated feature expansion and keep demand assumptions explicit.
- The user may consider upgrading to a paid AI plan after a useful result; this is not purchase authorization. Do not add any subscription or other cost before a clear value demonstration and the user's decision.
- No paid tools, purchases, publishing, or financial commitments without explicit approval.
- The user has not confirmed their residence, so channel eligibility and payout feasibility remain open.
- A local Windows check found basic text-to-speech with one en-US female voice and Microsoft Clipchamp installed, but no Russian system voice or FFmpeg. Current tools do not expose control of Clipchamp, and voice quality has not been checked; do not claim to have delivered a finished video or Russian voiceover.
