## 2026-10-20 — AI-assisted lore videos remain a channel hypothesis

- **Decision:** Do not select a lore channel as the project's digital product. Keep it as an unvalidated creator-business hypothesis.
- **Rationale:** A 25-item public fan-community sample shows interest in lore content and repeated requests for accessible, accurate, listenable explanations. Strong competitors already cover cross-universe documentaries, deep dives, primers, humour, and immersive narration. The only promising gap so far—clear source/canon distinctions—has not been shown to be underserved or monetizable.
- **Constraints:** YouTube requires original viewer value and disallows generic mass-produced monetization; borrowed franchise images, clips, music, and long text excerpts still carry copyright risk. A YouTube policy update scheduled for 2027-02-01 raises the ad/Premium entry threshold for new creators.
- **Next evidence:** An English Tolkien script package has been prepared as a no-cost research/scripting demonstration, not as a channel or product choice. Before investing in further video production, compare the creator channel with remaining business models on demand evidence, time to revenue, recurring workload, and cost. No audio/video has been produced or published.
- **User value condition:** The user may consider a paid AI plan if the assistant demonstrates effectiveness. No subscription was purchased or authorized; first evidence should be a no-cost sample with measured time saved.
